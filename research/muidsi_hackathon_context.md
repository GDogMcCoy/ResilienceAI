# MUIDSI Hackathon Research Report

## Executive Summary

The **MU Institute for Data Science and Informatics (MUIDSI)** at the University of Missouri hosts an annual hackathon focused on leveraging AI and data science for social good. This report compiles findings about the hackathon's history, themes, winning projects, prize structures, sponsors, and what MUIDSI values in projects.

## 7. What MUIDSI Values in Projects

### Core Values Identified

#### 1. **Social Impact and "AI for Good"**
- Every hackathon theme emphasizes social good
- Winning projects address real-world problems affecting communities
- Focus on underserved populations (healthcare chatbot for underserved communities, assistance for visually impaired)

#### 2. **Interdisciplinary Collaboration**
- Teams from 9+ different departments encouraged
- Cross-pollination of ideas from engineering, life sciences, statistics, and informatics
- Real-world problems require diverse expertise

#### 3. **Technical Innovation**
- Use of cutting-edge AI/ML technologies (Generative AI, LLMs, Computer Vision)
- Integration of multiple technologies (BLIP-2, LLaMA-Vision, YOLO)
- Novel applications of existing tools to new problems

#### 4. **Real-World Applicability**
- Projects must solve practical, tangible problems
- Solutions should be implementable and scalable
- Focus on authentic problem statements from university departments (2026)

#### 5. **Technical Excellence**
- Proper use of advanced ML/AI methods
- Working prototypes, not just concepts
- 48-hour implementation challenge (2024)

#### 6. **Accessibility and Inclusivity**
- Projects that help marginalized or disadvantaged groups score well
- Voice-based interfaces for accessibility
- Healthcare solutions for underserved communities

#### 7. **Data-Driven Decision Making**
- Evidence-based solutions
- Analysis of complex datasets
- Predictive modeling for social benefit

## 11. Strategic Insights for Participants

### What Wins
1. **Clear Social Impact** - Projects that directly help people or communities
2. **Technical Sophistication** - Use of modern AI/ML tools and techniques
3. **Working Prototype** - Functional demonstration, not just slides
4. **Interdisciplinary Approach** - Teams with diverse backgrounds
5. **Accessibility Focus** - Solutions for disabled or underserved populations
6. **Real-World Data** - Analysis of authentic, complex datasets

### Emerging Trends
- Shift from "Generative AI" (2025) to "Agentic AI" (2026)
- Increased prize pools indicating growing importance
- Integration of real department-provided datasets
- Focus on AI agents and autonomous systems

### Recommended Approach
1. Form diverse teams (technical + domain expertise)
2. Identify a clear social problem with measurable impact
3. Leverage cutting-edge AI tools (LLMs, computer vision, etc.)
4. Build a working prototype, not just a concept
5. Focus on accessibility and underserved populations
6. Use real data and demonstrate practical applicability
