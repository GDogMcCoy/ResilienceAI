# MUIDSI Hackathon Winners Research Report

## Executive Summary

This report documents research on past winning projects from the **MU Institute for Data Science and Informatics (MUIDSI) Hackathon** competitions held at the University of Missouri-Columbia from 2023-2025. MUIDSI hackathons focus on "AI for Social Good" and "Generative AI for Social Good" themes, bringing together interdisciplinary teams to solve real-world challenges using artificial intelligence and data science.

---

## 1. Hackathon Overview

### What is MUIDSI?
The **MU Institute for Data Science and Informatics (MUIDSI)** is a research institute at the University of Missouri-Columbia that brings together faculty and students from multiple disciplines to advance data science, AI, and informatics research.

### Hackathon Format
- **Duration**: Typically 48 hours to 5 days
- **Team Size**: 2-3 members per team (with faculty advisor)
- **Theme**: "AI for Social Good" / "Generative AI for Social Good"
- **Participation**: Open to students from multiple departments
- **Presentation Format**: 5-minute pitch with one slide, followed by prototype demonstration

### Prize Structure (2025)
- 1st Prize: $1,000
- 2nd Prize: $600  
- 3rd Prize: $300

### Common Themes Across Winning Projects

#### 1. **Social Impact Focus**
All winning projects address genuine societal challenges:
- Healthcare accessibility (VisionAI, Voice-Based Healthcare Chatbot, Cancer Registry)
- Government transparency (BudgetCuts.AI)
- Education empowerment (MAGNA LECTA)
- Public policy analysis

#### 2. **Underserved Populations**
Winning teams frequently target vulnerable groups:
- Visually impaired individuals
- Underserved communities
- Cancer patients
- Students needing educational support

#### 3. **Generative AI Applications (2025 Trend)**
The 2025 hackathon specifically focused on Generative AI, with winners leveraging:
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Voice synthesis and recognition
- Real-time data analysis and visualization
- Conversational AI interfaces

#### 4. **Interdisciplinary Collaboration**
Successful teams combine expertise from:
- Computer Science/Engineering (technical implementation)
- Healthcare/Medicine (domain expertise)
- Social Sciences (understanding user needs)
- Data Science (analytics and modeling)

## 5. Judging Criteria & Success Factors

### Inferred Judging Criteria

Based on the hackathon format and winning projects, judges likely evaluate:

#### 1. **Social Impact (Primary Criterion)**
- Does the project address a genuine societal problem?
- Is the target population clearly identified?
- What is the potential scale of positive impact?

#### 2. **Technical Innovation**
- Novel application of AI/ML techniques
- Appropriate use of generative AI capabilities
- Technical feasibility and implementation quality

#### 3. **Interdisciplinary Approach**
- Integration of domain expertise (healthcare, policy, education)
- Collaboration across different academic backgrounds
- Holistic solution design

#### 4. **Presentation & Communication**
- Clear 5-minute pitch delivery
- Effective use of single-slide presentation
- Ability to articulate problem-solution fit

#### 5. **Prototype Quality**
- Working demonstration
- User interface/experience design
- Real-world applicability

### What Makes Projects Win

#### Pattern 1: Clear Problem-Solution Fit
Winning projects identify specific, measurable problems:
- VisionAI → Emergency assistance for visually impaired
- BudgetCuts.AI → Understanding government budget impact
- Voice-Based Healthcare Chatbot → Healthcare access barriers

#### Pattern 2: Demonstrable Prototype
Successful teams deliver working solutions within the compressed timeframe (48 hours to 5 days).

#### Pattern 3: Strong Narrative
Projects that tell a compelling story about who benefits and how tend to perform better.

#### Pattern 4: Technical Sophistication
Winners demonstrate advanced AI/ML capabilities appropriate to the hackathon theme.

## 7. Key Insights for Future Competitors

### Lessons from Past Winners

1. **Start with the Problem, Not the Technology**
   - All winning projects identified genuine social problems first
   - Technology selection followed from problem requirements

2. **Build Diverse Teams**
   - Winners consistently came from interdisciplinary backgrounds
   - Combine technical skills with domain expertise

3. **Focus on Accessibility**
   - Projects serving disabled or underserved populations perform well
   - Judges value inclusive design

4. **Create Working Prototypes**
   - 48-hour timeframe requires focused scope
   - Working demo beats perfect concept

5. **Practice Your Pitch**
   - 5-minute presentation is critical
   - Single slide must communicate value proposition clearly

6. **Leverage University Resources**
   - MUIDSI provides mentorship and technical resources
   - IBM partnership offers cloud credits and tools
   - Hellbender HPC cluster available for computation

## 9. Conclusion

The MUIDSI Hackathon series demonstrates the University of Missouri's commitment to applying AI and data science for social good. Winning projects consistently share characteristics:

- **Social impact focus** targeting underserved populations
- **Interdisciplinary teams** combining technical and domain expertise  
- **Working prototypes** delivered under tight deadlines
- **Clear communication** of problem-solution fit

For teams preparing for future MUIDSI hackathons, the formula for success is clear: identify a genuine social problem, assemble a diverse team, leverage available AI/ML tools, and deliver a working solution with a compelling narrative.
